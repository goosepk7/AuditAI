How to lower your Liberty Mutual bill

Updated October 2026

Liberty Mutual and companies like it often cut prices for customers who ask, especially ones who mention leaving. A 15 minute call commonly saves 10 to 30 percent.

Who to call

Call the customer service number on your bill. When the phone menu asks why you're calling, say "cancel service". That usually reaches the retention team, who are allowed to give discounts. Have your current monthly price ready.

Script

"Hi, I've been a customer for a while and I'm currently paying [your amount] a month. That's more than I want to spend, and I've gotten quotes from other insurers for similar coverage at a lower price. I'd like to stay, but I need my bill closer to [about 25% less]. What promotions or loyalty discounts can you apply to my account today?"

If they say no

  • "Is there a cheaper plan that still covers what I actually use?"
  • "Can you check for any autopay, paperless, bundle, or loyalty discounts?"
  • "Could you transfer me to the retention or loyalty department?"
  • If nothing works: "Okay, I'll think about switching," and call back in a few days. A different rep often says yes.

Insurance tip

Get two or three online quotes first (about 10 minutes each). Ask about raising your deductible, bundling home and auto, and low-mileage or safe-driver discounts.

Before you hang up

  • Ask for the new monthly price, how long it lasts, and whether there's a contract.
  • Write down the rep's name and the date.
