How do I download my statements?

Sign in on your bank's website (the desktop site is easiest), open the account, and look for Download or Export near your transactions. Pick CSV (sometimes called "Spreadsheet", "Excel" or "Comma delimited") and a date range covering the last 90 days.

  • Chase: open the account, click the download icon above transactions, choose "Spreadsheet (Excel, CSV)".
  • Bank of America: open the account, click "Download", choose "Microsoft Excel" format.
  • Wells Fargo: Account Activity, then "Download Account Activity", choose "Comma Delimited".
  • Capital One: open the account, "View more transactions", then "Download Transactions", choose CSV.
  • Citi, Discover, American Express: on the activity or statements page, choose "Download", then CSV.
  • Only have PDFs? Upload the PDF statements. Text-based PDFs work; scanned images don't.

Menus change from time to time; if you can't find it, search your bank's help pages for "download transactions CSV".
