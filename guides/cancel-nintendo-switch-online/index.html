How to cancel Nintendo Switch Online

Updated October 2026

Cancelling Nintendo Switch Online takes a few minutes. Here's the fastest way, plus a script to use if they make you call or chat. Your access usually continues until the end of the period you already paid for.

Steps

  1. Sign in and go to the cancel page: accounts.nintendo.com.
  2. Look for Cancel, Cancel membership or Manage plan. You may need to click past one or two "are you sure?" offers.
  3. Finish until you see a confirmation screen or get a confirmation email. Save it.
  4. If the page tells you to call or chat instead, use the script below.

Good to know

  • If you signed up inside an iPhone or Android app, you're probably billed by Apple or Google, not by the company. Cancel it in your iPhone's Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or in the Google Play Store under Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Phone or chat script

"Hi, I'd like to cancel my Nintendo Switch Online subscription, effective today. Please don't transfer me to another offer; I've decided to cancel. Can you confirm the cancellation and send me a confirmation number or email?"

If they offer a discount: "Thanks, but please go ahead and cancel." (Or, if the discount is worth it to you, ask them to put it in writing.)

After you cancel

  • Save the confirmation email or number.
  • Check your next statement. If you're charged again, dispute it with your bank or card company and mention the confirmation.
