How to Get an Overdraft Fee Refunded

Updated October 2026

Banks and card companies refund many fees on the first ask, especially if you rarely get them. It takes one short call or chat.

Who to contact

Call the number on the back of your card, or use your bank's chat in the app. Have the date and amount of the overdraft fee ready.

Script

"Hi, I noticed a overdraft fee of [amount] on my account on [date]. I've been a customer for a while and this isn't typical for me. Could you please refund it as a courtesy?"

If they say no

  • "I understand. Could you refund half of it?"
  • "Is there anything on my account that would make me eligible, like setting up direct deposit or alerts?"

Stop it happening again

Turn on low-balance alerts, and ask the bank to decline transactions instead of charging overdraft fees ("opt out of overdraft coverage").
